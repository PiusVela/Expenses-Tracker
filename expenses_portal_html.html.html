About Angulo's Expense & Income Tracker

A modern expense and income tracking application designed specifically for Ugandan Shillings (UGX).

Features:

  • Track expenses and Income in UGX
  • Edit and Delete records
  • Tap-to-reveal balance privacy
  • Beautiful charts and visualizations
  • Full Financial Reports (Income & Expense)
  • Specific Category/Source Reports
  • PDF report generation
  • Real-time Firebase sync
  • Dark/Light Mode toggle
  • Budget Tracking
  • Savings Goals
  • Recurring Transactions
  • Financial Insights
  • Data Export/Import
  • Search functionality
  • Notifications
